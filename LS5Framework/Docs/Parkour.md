# Parkour

Parkour is a modular ability under `Abilities/Parkour` in Sonic, Adventure Sonic, and Tails. Hold Ability Slot 7 (R1 / F by default) near an airborne wall contact, or press it while regularly attached to a vertical wall. Jump kicks away; releasing Parkour returns to airborne movement. The character loadout can assign another slot, and both options menus expose the same shared slot bindings.

## Movement

- Horizontal wall-parallel entry speed selects run or cling before any assistance is granted. The default decision band spans 24 to 36 around the center speed of 30. Entry at or below 24 always clings and entry at or above 36 always runs. Within the band, no input or input normal to the wall biases toward cling, while input whose wall-relative direction is increasingly parallel lowers the speed required to run. The parallel bias begins at a 45% share and reaches full strength at 75%. An existing run observes its gravity-horizontal, wall-parallel speed before collision sliding and transitions to cling after 0.12 seconds below 12. The delay prevents a single low-speed sample from stopping the run immediately.
- Parkour can catch a wall directly from Fall, Jump, Bounce, Bounce Rebound, Stomp, Skydive, and Spin Kick. Entering Parkour clears any active bounce or rebound attack state. Pressing Parkour while regularly attached to a gravity-vertical wall converts the grounded wall alignment into Parkour, preserving surface-relative velocity and using the same speed-and-input decision between wall run and wall cling.
- Runs preserve signed parallel momentum as the wall normal changes, rotating velocity into each accepted face instead of re-projecting it. Qualifying wall-run collisions are resolved by Parkour after `move_and_slide`: pre-slide parallel and gravity-vertical speeds are reconstructed on the accepted collision face, and ordinary airborne wall friction is skipped. Adhesion pressure uses the gravity-horizontal projection of the wall normal, preventing walls sloped toward a ceiling or floor from adding upward or downward velocity. Vertical travel still follows the wall-relative up axis, preserving contact across the slope without changing its gravity-axis speed. This preserves intentional acceleration or deceleration while preventing collision projection and the later friction pass from bleeding speed at sharp concave joins, including the inside of a cylinder. Optional entry and kick speed targets default to zero, disabling top-ups without limiting existing speed.
- Fresh wall-run catches redirect 15% of gravity-horizontal speed aimed into the wall onto the selected wall-run tangent, capped at 5 additional speed units. This compensates for oblique entry projection without granting speed on clings, outward catches, or remembered repeat catches. Surface-history strength scales the conversion along with the other entry assistance.
- Fresh wall-run entry gains 12% wall-parallel speed capped at 5 additional speed units and 10% upward speed capped at 2 units. The parallel boost follows the current signed travel direction. Downward entry velocity is reduced by 25%. Surface similarity scales the granted assistance, and repeat catches do not grant either boost again.
- Dash panels triggered during a wall run project their rotated launch direction onto the current wall plane. With `stop_momentum`, the projected panel direction and strength replace prior wall-run velocity. With `IgnorePriorVertical`, existing parallel speed is retained, prior vertical speed is replaced, and panel speed is added. With `FullyAdditiveWithMinLaunch`, the panel impulse adds to both current components and enforces the configured minimum launch speed. A short configurable wall-run hold permits steep launches without changing the panel's direction to meet the normal horizontal run threshold. The panel does not move the runner to its center or convert wall contact into grounded attachment. Its configured movement and action locks, camera alignment, speed lock, and temporary maximum-speed override remain available during the run.
- Run gravity starts at 20% and returns smoothly to full gravity. Unassisted entry speeds from 18 to 70 map to assistance durations from 0.45 to 1.3 seconds.
- Cling parallel velocity settles with a 0.12-second half-life toward stick-directed motion, up to 3 units per second. Existing upward velocity is damped toward zero with a 0.35-second half-life, and clings do not receive the wall-run upward entry boost. A cling entered directly from regular surface attachment skips this additional upward damping but applies at least 4 times normal gravity while rising. Grounded entry speed is not stored for kick mirroring. Ordinary cling gravity starts at 20%, waits 0.35 seconds, then grows to full gravity over 1 second. Transitioning from a run retains elapsed contact time and uses ordinary cling damping. The ordinary terminal fall speed still applies.
- Vertical calculations use gravity-up. Wall contact never sets regular attachment, changes physics-up to the wall normal, or replenishes airborne abilities through landing logic.
- Wall detection first accepts eligible `move_and_slide` contacts, then uses 24 evenly spaced gravity-relative rays with a central sphere sweep fallback. Ray queries accept back faces and correct returned normals against their probe direction, which keeps CSG collision winding from making detection orientation-dependent. An established run also probes ahead by 1.5 physics frames of travel, capped at 3 units by default. The upcoming face is selected before movement, rotating preserved parallel velocity and contact pressure onto the curve instead of allowing the character to continue tangent to the previous face. Continuity is evaluated by the change in surface normal without applying initial-catch outward-velocity restrictions. Normal changes along the travel direction distinguish outward from inward curvature. Outward turns up to 45 degrees and inward turns up to 75 degrees retain contact; sharper outward turns detach after the normal contact grace window.
- Jump takes priority over airborne homing and jump-dash routes while caught. Wall-run and wall-cling kicks have independent outward, upward, parallel top-up, steering, input-lock, input-influence, sustain, and recatch settings. Both profiles default to 60 outward speed and 35 upward speed while retaining faster upward momentum and existing parallel velocity. Movement input is fully locked for the first 0.35 seconds. Input back toward the kicked wall is then redirected away and smoothly restored over 0.12 to 0.32 seconds based on actual outward strength; parallel and outward input remain responsive during this recovery. The awarded outward and upward speeds are continuously sustained for independently configurable 0.18 and 0.08 second windows. Sustaining minimum component speeds avoids frame-rate-dependent additive impulses. The kicked surface also has a short recatch lockout and separation requirement, while other wall orientations remain eligible.
- Wall-run and airborne wall-cling kicks mirror captured entry speed into their launch strength through independent settings. Grounded-to-cling entries do not use entry-speed or entry-vertical-speed mirroring. Wall-run kicks carry current gravity-relative vertical speed separately by direction: upward speed is multiplied before comparison with the kick's upward target (1.0 by default), while 20% of downward speed reduces that target, no lower than zero. The captured entry-vertical bonus is then added separately. Cling kicks instead add outward speed from the current gravity-relative cling slide: 25% of upward slide speed or 10% of downward slide speed by default. The last vertical wall speed is retained for either type of coyote kick. Entry mirroring and slide bonuses remain subject to surface-history and optional tic-tac degradation.
- Releasing Parkour or losing wall contact records the last valid wall normal, mode, run speed, and animation side for 0.12 seconds. Pressing Jump during this coyote window consumes the snapshot and performs the same kick as direct contact. Invalid interaction exits, grounded handoffs, and completed kicks do not create another coyote window.
- Barrier Blast depletion pauses during runs and clings. Wall runs build gauge through the standard speed-based fill curve, while wall clings do not generate charge.
- Wall cling entry, active downward sliding, cling kicks, and wall-run kicks use the matching `Parkour_` ability sounds. The slide cue begins after the configured slide delay, uses a continuous forward loop only while descending with confirmed wall contact, and stops on kick, detach, or landing. Its volume rises linearly from -12 dB to 0 dB, reaching the configured maximum at 60 units per second by default. Each sound and its volume range are configurable on the Parkour ability.
- During a wall run, the camera target pans horizontally toward the open side of the wall. The pan is projected onto camera-right, so a wall on the player's right produces a leftward world-space pan without adding unwanted forward or vertical framing. Its distance ramps with wall-run speed and blends smoothly on entry, side changes, and exit.
- Wall kicks can request a controller-only camera yaw assist toward their resultant planar launch direction. The correction considers outward speed, surface-strength damping, current view error, preserved parallel momentum, and near-backward disorientation risk. It is capped at 18 degrees by default, fades over a short 0.4-second window, yields progressively to right-stick input, and cancels on strong manual look, keyboard or mouse input, camera constraints, or disabled camera controls.
- Slow clings can hand off to ledge mantle when its input, approach, and clearance requirements pass. Runs do not automatically mantle. A ledge candidate must have an unobstructed opening immediately above its edge, a clear swept-character route from the current position into the hang, and clear rise and inward mantle sweeps. Walls that continue above a remotely detected top surface, overhanging ceilings, and geometry between the player and the ledge reject the grab.
- Bounce, Bounce Rebound, Stomp, Skydive, and airborne Tornado Kick may enter Parkour and use the normal horizontal wall-parallel speed threshold to select wall run or cling. Catching the wall ends the previous action and clears bounce-family attack state. A successful wall attachment refreshes Jump Dash and Tornado Kick eligibility and clears the Jump Dash recent-use timer. The subsequent wall kick consumes neither ability and does not perform another refresh.
- Ledge grab owns animation and movement while it is active. A ledge jump blocks parkour catches and parkour animation handoffs for 0.75 seconds, preventing the held Parkour input from replacing the ledge jump.
- A qualifying floor collision immediately ends a run or cling, projects velocity onto the floor, commits surface attachment and landing state, and clears the airborne action. Upward velocity no longer prevents an existing parkour interaction from recognizing actual floor contact.
- Wall kicks enter the standard airborne jump attack state until landing. Their left-run, right-run, and cling animation resources use `_jump-ball-enabled=false` in Resource > Name, keeping the character mesh visible while the attack remains active.
- Pressing Parkour within the configured pre-landing window buffers a landing roll. Speed recovery requires the configured minimum airborne time when the player jumps or starts another action in the air; simply dropping off a ledge bypasses this minimum, even if a ground roll continues into the fall. Passive drops also use a separate lower impact-speed threshold so short falls can enter the roll. A qualifying impact is remembered for the configured post-landing window; late rolls use the airborne duration and action status captured at landing. Both activation paths respect the speed-benefit cooldown. Surface angle, impact speed, tangential speed, recovery fraction, and recovery cap are configured on Parkour, with separate overrides in character scenes. Qualifying rolls still play `Landing_Roll.wav` and enter `CMD_LANDING_ROLL` when speed recovery is unavailable. The debug view reports whether the air-time gate applied and the exact rejected condition, including surface angle, impact speed, tangential speed, air-time, cooldown, or speed gain.
- The Movement section of the player debug view retains the latest qualifying landing-roll calculation. It reports whether recovery was granted, recovered speed, base recovery strength, combo effectiveness, recovery cap, captured tangential speed, captured and required airtime, and the remaining and configured cooldown.

## Repetition

The most recent kicked outward normal is compared with the current normal. Granted strength interpolates linearly in angle from 2% at 0 degrees to 100% at 90 degrees. Angles above 90 degrees receive full strength, so opposing-wall tic-tacs remain strong by default. Similarity influence fades over 3.5 seconds or 175 units of displacement, whichever removes it first. Earlier kicked normals are replaced on the next kick to avoid penalizing ordinary alternating tic-tacs.

Recent catches are tracked separately for 2 seconds within 8 units. Returning to the same facing contact does not grant another entry boost or restart the run assistance window. Cling slide elapsed time also survives these recatches. Releasing the input does not clear either history.

Landing, bouncing from the ground, and receiving credit for destroying an enemy refresh all parkour traversal state. This clears surface similarity, catch history, tic-tac degradation, recatch restrictions, post-kick input influence, and sustained kick components. Enemy refreshes occur at the authoritative defeat event, including kills that do not use the contact-bounce response.

Optional tic-tac degradation is disabled by default. When enabled, the first 8 airborne kicks have no additional chain penalty. Each later kick removes 15 percentage points of strength, down to a 25% minimum. The outward separation minimum remains available. Landing, death, and respawn reset the chain.

## Character Setup

Add `parkour_ability.gd` to a node under `Abilities`. The Inspector groups contact detection, wall-run assistance, cling slide, separate wall-run and wall-cling kick profiles, surface history, and tic-tac degradation settings. Script defaults use the tuning previously stored on Sonic Neo Adventure's Parkour node, so new Parkour ability nodes inherit that behavior without scene overrides. Numeric and vector variables are explicitly typed; exported parameters have Inspector documentation.

Run and cling commands use placeholder states in the supplied Sonic, Sonic Neo Adventure, and Tails animation graphs. The model faces along a run and into a cling. Replace each placeholder node's clip when the dedicated animation is available.

The jump-ball effect reads `_jump-ball-enabled` from the active AnimationTree node's Resource > Name arguments. Set `_jump-ball-enabled=false` on an animation node to suppress the jump-ball mesh without disabling its attack state.

The landing roll uses a dedicated `CMD_LANDING_ROLL` state in the Sonic, Sonic Neo Adventure, and Tails graphs. Sonic Neo Adventure uses `Land_Roll`; Sonic and Tails retain `RollFloor` as a placeholder. Each graph owns the roll's lifetime through an at-end transition into `GROUND_MACHINE`, and ordinary grounded animation selection yields while the command is pending or active.

Sonic Neo Adventure uses its dedicated `WallRun_Left`, `WallRun_Right`, `WallKick_Left`, `WallKick_Right`, and `WallKick_Cling` clips. Its cling command enters a nested state machine that plays `WallCling_Enter` once, crossfades into `WallCling_Loop`, and remains there while the cling is active. Each wall-kick state transitions to `FallBlend` at the end of its clip and can be interrupted immediately by a grounded transition. Detachment retains a separate short placeholder before `FallBlend`.

Wall kicks select one of three commands: left-wall run, right-wall run, or cling. Each has its own placeholder animation node so the final clips can be assigned independently. A completed wall-kick clip hands off to `FallBlend`, with 0.3 seconds as the fallback when its playback length is unavailable. Releasing Parkour or losing contact uses the separate `CMD_WALL_DETACH` placeholder, with a 0.12-second fallback before the same handoff. Landing, catching another wall, or activating an unrelated action cancels the pending fall transition so it cannot overwrite the newer animation.

Wall runs support separate commands for walls on the character's left and right relative to the gravity-relative run direction. The original wall-run command remains as a fallback when either side command is empty. Reversing direction along the wall switches sides and requests the matching command.

Wall-run playback speed ranges from 0.75 to 1.5 by default and is smoothed with a 0.08-second half-life. Combined mode uses whichever normalized component is stronger: horizontal wall-parallel speed relative to 60 units per second, or absolute vertical speed relative to 30. Horizontal-only and vertical-only modes are also available. The modifier uses the shared movement time-scale node and applies only while the Parkour action is in wall-run mode.

The script is explicitly included in the Characters pack export list. Shared controller hooks handle input priority, movement contact, attachment exclusion, visual orientation, and depletion preservation.

## Manual Verification

Godot was not launched during implementation. Runtime tuning remains to be checked in a playable build:

1. Approach a flat wall below 24, between 24 and 36, and above 36 wall-parallel speed. The low entry should always cling and the high entry should always run. In the middle band, compare no input, input into the wall, diagonal input, and input parallel to the wall; only the middle-band result should change with input bias.
2. Catch a wall from Bounce descent, Bounce Rebound, Stomp, and both Skydive phases. Confirm each source exits cleanly into either run or cling and leaves no bounce, stomp, rebound, or skydive state active. While regularly attached and running across a vertical wall, press Parkour and confirm attachment converts immediately using the current surface normal and lateral speed. Repeat at low speed and with input into the wall to confirm a cling is selected. Confirm pressing Parkour while attached to a floor or ordinary slope does nothing.
3. Compare rising and falling entry, then wait through the full run and cling gravity ramps. Force actual gravity-horizontal velocity below 12 during a run and confirm it becomes a cling only after the 0.12-second delay. Release/rehold on the same wall and cross mesh seams; assistance must not restart.
4. Enter a wall run at low and high parallel speeds. Confirm the signed horizontal entry boost adds 12% up to its 5-unit cap without reversing direction, then give the wall-run and cling kick profiles visibly different outward and upward values. Kick from both modes and confirm each uses its matching profile, retains parallel momentum, and preserves jump priority with a nearby homing target.
5. Immediately hold back toward the kicked wall after weak and strong kicks. Confirm the initial full input lock, then verify that inward steering returns smoothly, lasts longer for stronger kicks, and does not suppress parallel or outward steering after the lock.
6. Set noticeably different upward and outward sustain times. Confirm that each velocity component remains at least at its awarded launch speed for its own window, then releases to normal air physics without accumulating speed each frame.
7. Compare similarly facing, perpendicular, and opposing walls. Alternate opposing walls beyond eight kicks with degradation off, then enable degradation and repeat. Land and repeat to verify reset.
8. Run into a floor, lose the wall at a real gap, and traverse both the inside and outside of cylinders. Use a faceted concave cylinder with sharp inward joins and confirm reported and traveled parallel speed remain constant without per-face friction loss. Confirm inward curves remain attached through 75 degrees, outward changes remain attached through 45 degrees, and sharper exterior corners detach after the short contact grace window. Approach the same outward face while already moving away from it and confirm a fresh wall catch is still rejected.
9. Catch near a ledge while running, then while clinging with input toward it. Only the eligible cling should mantle. Place another wall or low ceiling between the player and a valid ledge, and place a horizontal surface behind a taller foreground wall; each obscured candidate should be rejected without moving the player.
10. Repeat under redirected gravity, with Barrier Blast active, after damage/respawn, and with remapped controls in both menus.
11. Check scene-specific contact reach against collision size, controller steering, and animation transitions. Moving or rotating wall geometry requires additional playtesting.
12. Kick from left-wall runs, right-wall runs, and clings. Confirm each reaches its matching placeholder state and then `FallBlend`. Release Parkour and lose wall contact without jumping; both should use `CMD_WALL_DETACH`, while touching the floor should proceed directly into landing animation.
13. Enter a ledge grab from a cling and hold Parkour through the ledge jump. Confirm the grab and jump animations remain uninterrupted, wall catches are blocked for 0.75 seconds, and parkour becomes available afterward. Use Jump Dash or Tornado Kick, attach to a nearby wall, and confirm both become eligible immediately. Use one after the wall kick and confirm the kick itself does not refresh it a second time.
14. Enter a cling, wait for its downward slide, and kick away. Confirm the enter and cling-jump cues play once, the slide cue loops only during active descent, and it stops immediately on exit. Repeat from a wall run and confirm its distinct jump cue plays.
15. Wall-run with walls on each side at slow and fast speeds. Confirm the camera pans toward the open side, remains screen-horizontal under redirected gravity, changes sides smoothly, and recenters after detaching.
16. On a controller, wall-kick with the camera already aligned, perpendicular, and nearly backward to the launch. Confirm the assist remains subtle, is strongest for a fast sideward departure, and avoids a large reversal. Repeat while moving the right stick, then with keyboard and mouse input; manual input should suppress or cancel the assist, and non-controller kicks should not activate it.
17. Enter a wall run at shallow and steep inward approach angles with equal wall-parallel speed. Confirm only the fresh run receives the 15% inward-speed conversion, the bonus never exceeds 5 units, and cling, outward, and remembered repeat catches receive none.
18. Press Parkour shortly before and within 0.2 seconds after a hard flat landing. Confirm both timings recover speed from the incoming tangential velocity, play `Landing_Roll.wav`, and enter `CMD_LANDING_ROLL`. Repeat outside both windows, on a slope steeper than 15 degrees, below 35 impact speed, and below 8 tangential speed; none should roll. Confirm the animation returns through its authored at-end transition and that jumping or otherwise leaving the floor interrupts it normally.
19. Release Parkour or run beyond a wall edge, then press Jump within and outside the 0.12-second wall-kick coyote window. Confirm only the timely press kicks from the remembered surface with the correct run/cling profile and animation side. Confirm wall kicks damage valid targets while the character mesh remains visible and the jump-ball mesh stays hidden for all three wall-kick animations.
